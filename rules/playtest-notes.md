# Driven: Playtest notes

Split out of rules.md on Nov 15, 2026. Playtest findings, change log, and session logs live here; rules.md holds only the rules.

## Playtest notes

**Where the rules stand (Nov 10).** The chapters in rules.md are the current rules. These started as trial rules and are now settled after the voice session, the simulator and four Gemini sessions: Second Wind, guns at Disadvantage in melee, Dark zones, reduced mook spill, the 2-Harm mook cap, villain turns and gang size scaling with the party, the four Stress rules, strange powers costing Stress, power flaws, and the slot system.

### Resolved Nov 10

- **Protector and Detective Drive.** The Protector now earns from any attack against them, hit or miss. The Detective also earns when an ally hits using the Edge from their Read. The Protector went from 0.29 to 0.62 Drive a round; win rates didn't move.
- **Guard 15 enemies.** Kept, with GM advice under Fights (Hard-to-hit enemies): one per fight at most, and always a way to get Edge or Advantage.
- **The Specialist against guns.** Kit is now in the simulator. Smoke cuts Patchwork's chance of going Down against gunmen from 28% to 23%, and the Kit text now says to take Smoke or Armor when guns are expected. Prepared's trap now ignores Toughness, which lifts parties with a Specialist about 4 points.
- **Already Broken** starts each Night with 4 Drive. In the simulator Rook gets 1.7 Signatures a fight instead of 1.1, and Nightjar (who already runs hot) Breaks in 70% of fights instead of 18%. A real trade, which is the point.
- **Lethality.** A hero is only ever Dying when an enemy hits them while they are already Down, and the GM announces that move first. Since Nov 12, death itself is decided at the table: a Dying hero who isn't stabilized is left for dead unless their player says otherwise.
- **Fight length.** A case's main villain has 10 Harm (8 for a smaller one). The Cannery now runs 4.7 rounds with a 90.6% win rate.
- **Toughness villains.** If a Detective's Read asks about the weakness, the hit using that Edge ignores Toughness. With guns, Mighty, the Taser and the trap, every party now has an answer to armor.
- **The Order Trait.** Session 1 showed Order giving Edge on almost every roll. Its Steady perk is now: name one step of your plan, and the roll for that step has Edge, once per scene.

### Still to watch

- **Parties with no fighter** (Patchwork, the Ledger, a third non-fighter) win only 38% of the Cannery. That's intended: they should win the case and pick their ground first. Check it at a table.
- **A villain fighting alone** goes down in about 3.3 rounds (Nov 12 run). With 12 Harm it is 3.9 rounds, but the weakest party drops from 56% to 38%. If it feels short in play, give solo villains 12 Harm.
- **Bruisers are a small lever.** In the simulator, making one Saints group bruisers takes the Cannery from 93.4% wins in 4.4 rounds to 89.4% in 4.6, and the weakest party from 45% to 33%. Making every group bruisers in a gang-only fight: 99.4% in 2.5 rounds becomes 97.9% in 2.9. A pair of bruisers is weaker than three Saints with a pistol, so they come in threes. Adding a bruiser group on top of the usual gang, with the Butcher there, drops the average to 73%: too much.
- **Gang-only fights are short** (2.5 rounds, 99% wins) mostly because the gang runs when half of it is down. If they feel too easy, give the gang a reason to stay, or a lieutenant.
- **Objectives can be very fast.** In the simulator, three non-fighters finish the Cannery's three steps in about 2.4 rounds. Limiting the team to one finished step a round makes that 3.3 rounds. A GM who contests the steps may make the limit unnecessary. Watch it.
- **The Leader needs a fighter or an Objective.** Presence attacks deal no Harm, so a Leader with two non-fighters wins a straight slugfest only 7 to 21% of the time in the simulator, but 76 to 93% when the fight has an Objective. Beside a fighter the Leader is about level with the other archetypes.
- **The new Order perk and Already Broken** haven't been played yet.
- **A Specialist with Gadgeteer** carries 5 belt tools. If one hero is answering every problem in a fight, limit belt tools to one use per turn.
- **A 4-segment Scheme clock** with beats on ticks 1, 2 and 3. Try it at the next live session. In the simulator it puts 2 to 4 heroes inside both targets at Complexity + 2, but the Scheme beats a solo hero in 40 to 51% of cases.
- **Burner phones** give almost nothing under the team's one no-roll clue: the extra use only turns into Edge on a lead.
- **Nightjar Breaks** in 56 to 77% of two-hero Cannery fights in the simulator: the hostage clock's 2 Stress, her strange power's 1 Stress, and the Butcher's Presence attack against her Poise of 11.
- **Session 3 questions** 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 20, 23, 24 and 26 are still open.

### GM practice (from the voice session)

- When a clock or threat is about to resolve, say "this round" or "next turn" precisely.
- Show Harm as boxes filled ("5 of 8 taken"), on sheets and in narration.
- A complication has to fit the fiction. A chain put on tonight isn't rusted.
- Give most fights a goal besides a knockout.

### Session 3

**Table errors.** The GM was a weaker model, and the audit found five errors. It charged costs on Impact 1 attack hits, kept a Bruised box a breather should have cleared, let the Brawler's Drive trigger fire more than once a round, gave a lieutenant extra turns and extra free Presence attacks, and never applied Danger Sense's +1 Guard. With those corrected, heroes hit 61% of the time and enemies 55%.

**Simulator (two reports, Nov 14).**

- **The case economy.** With 2 Patrol actions per hero, three heroes crack the case on Night 1 in 97% of cases. With a shared pool equal to the number of heroes, they crack it on Night 2 in 95%. The Patrol pool replaces the per-hero budget.
- **The alley fight** (Cyrus, Bulwark and Patchwork against Sparks and two Saints) runs about 3 rounds, with 0.50 Downs per fight at 4 Harm boxes. That is short of the 4 to 6 round target. A full group of three Saints adds about half a round.
- **Scheme beats at 1, 3 and 5** add about half a beat per case and change nothing else.

**Closed.**

- **A fifth Harm box for all heroes:** heroes stay at 4. Five boxes halved Downs (0.50 to 0.26 per fight) but barely changed the win rate.
- **The new Danger Sense ability** (once per fight, Disadvantage on one attack): rejected. It gave no gain over +1 Guard.
- **Toughness negating physical hazards:** rejected. A Hazard costs a flat 1 Harm.
- **Splitting up as a Patrol rule:** dropped.

### Change log

- **After Session 1 (voice):** the gun bonus only applies on Impact 2 or better. Mook attacks cap at 2 Harm, applied last. Heroes got gear and powers. Hazards hurt enemies too. Advantage and Edge stack.
- **Difficulty pass (simulator):** an Impact 3 drops two mooks instead of one per Impact. Villain turns and gang size scale with the party.
- **Powers pass:** the slot system replaced the Gifted and Equipped packages. Strange powers cost Stress and need a flaw. Armored and Invulnerability became always-on strange powers. Street powers were rebalanced, and Flairs, gear and Assets were added.
- **Balance pass (Nov 9, simulator):** defenses went from 10 + stat to 12 + stat, case villains to 8 Harm, lieutenants to 5, and a lone villain gets one turn per hero. Before: heroes hit 80 to 90%, won 93 to 100%, and fights lasted 2 to 3 rounds. After: about 63% hits and 4 to 4.5 rounds.
- **Rulings from the Gemini sessions (Nov 9 and 10):** Impact tops out at 3, and bonuses add Harm, not Impact. A Jab never gets Impact 2 bonuses. An always-on power doesn't work against its Allergy. Toughness applies before Invulnerability. Clocks tick last in a round. Scheme Stress only hits the hero whose roll caused the tick. Unstable misfires mostly hit the scene. A Down hero gets up at Wounded after the fight. Seeing an ally go Down costs Stress once per fight. A single hit stops at Down. Breaking while Down waits. Healing Factor heals under fire for 1 Stress.
- **Drive and Mighty (Nov 10, simulator):** heroes start each Night with 2 Drive, because they earn 0.3 to 0.8 a round, not 1. Average win went from 90% to 93% and the weakest party from 17% to 37%, with about one Signature per fight. Mighty ignores Toughness, which lifted the weakest party from 17% to 32%. Together: 93% average, weakest party 45%.
- **Tested and rejected:** wider Impact bands (no effect), Mighty with +1 Harm on any hit (armor ate it), starting with 3 Drive (too comfortable), Healing Factor that only works when nobody hurt you (it never worked), and Stress on a miss (never adopted).
- **Method:** Gemini invents dice and simulator tables unless it is handed a numbered dice bank, and every log needs an audit. Use the simulator for numbers and a live session for feel.
- **Open-questions pass (Nov 10, simulator):** Specialist Kit (Taser, Flashbang, Smoke) and Prepared are now simulated. Changes: Protector and Detective Drive triggers widened, Prepared's trap and a Read weakness ignore Toughness, case villains 10 Harm, Already Broken starts at 4 Drive, the Order perk reworked, and GM advice for hard-to-hit enemies and killing blows. Result across all 56 three-hero parties: the Cannery 90.6% wins in 4.7 rounds (weakest party 38%), the gun lieutenant 92.5% in 3.9 rounds, and every hero averages about one Signature a fight. Kit with Edge was tested and rejected (too small to be worth a rule).
- **Several named enemies (Nov 11, Stephen):** a fight can have more than one villain or lieutenant. Villains keep the usual turns; a lieutenant always gets one turn per round. The simulator and the Encounter lab model this; a single villain's fight is unchanged.
- **Audit pass (Nov 11, Stephen):** Trait perks that fired on nearly every roll are now once per scene or once per round. Named enemies can lose only one turn per fight. Case villains have 6 Stress, and Breaking no longer ends a fight. Patrol has a budget. GM moves, Defeat and Objectives were written down. Light melee, knives and heavy melee were rebalanced. Not run through the simulator.
- **Table and review pass (Nov 12, Stephen):** in a played session a hero reached Fraying and Sleep It Off wiped it, so Sleep It Off now clears 3 Stress and Show Up clears 1. A missed theory can go in anyway and hit a trap, so the Night never stalls. Objective steps pay as they go. Complexity scales with the team. Levitation no longer costs Stress every round. The GM moves were rewritten. Not run through the simulator.
- **Death, the Leader and the Insidious (Nov 12, Stephen):** death is decided at the table, never by the dice. A sixth archetype, the Leader, leads with Presence. A new villain role, the Insidious, does the same for villains. Not run through the simulator.
- **Core powers (Nov 12, Stephen):** twelve powers are core (six street, six strange). The rest stay in the book on "More powers" lists as trial rules. No power text changed.
- **Simulator pass (Nov 12):** the simulator now plays today's rules: the weapon changes, Grit your teeth, villain Stress and Breaking, the taser against named enemies, Objectives and the hostage clock, and the Leader. Across all 56 three-hero parties, the Cannery with the Butcher at 8 Harm ran 96.5% wins in 3.8 rounds, under the 4 to 6 round target, so the trial rule "a villain with Toughness has 2 fewer Harm" was dropped and the Butcher is back to 10. With 10 Harm: 93.4% wins in 4.4 rounds, weakest party 45%. Turning on the Cannery's Objective lifts the weakest party to 76% (92.6% average, 4.4 rounds, 42% of wins by Objective). Grit your teeth is worth about 1.5 points on average and 9 for the weakest party. Light-melee Jabs and villain Stress barely move the totals. The gun lieutenant: 94.7% in 3.6 rounds.
- **Bruisers (Nov 12):** a second mook type with 2 Harm boxes, for harder gangs. Making every mook 2 Harm was simulated and rejected: fights ran 0.5 rounds longer, but the weakest party fell from 45% to 31% and "any hit drops a mook" was lost.
- **Session 3 pass (Nov 14, Stephen):** an attack at Impact 1 never carries a cost, Bruised clears at the end of every scene, and archetype Drive triggers and lieutenants are spelled out as once per round. Other clarifications: Hazard types, Stress off the cost menu at 8, grenades thrown into an adjacent zone, the taser gun, the ambush's one Edge, Danger Sense on the sheet, Thrill Breaking, locked power abilities, and accomplices. New trial rules: the Patrol pool, clues and leads, the team's one no-roll clue, the bike as Edge, interrogation, Down and Stress (Breaking while Down no longer waits), Dying leaves a mark, one deadline per case, and Scheme beats at 1, 3 and 5.
