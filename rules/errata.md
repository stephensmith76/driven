# Errata: changes since the printed rules

Changes made to the rules since the printed copy, each with where to find it and the exact wording. The printed copy is the version headed "Nov 9, 2026" (first published Nov 10, 2026). If yours is newer, skip any entry it already has. The current rules are always the Rules link in the footer.

## 1. Guns and armor (Nov 10)

Armor now works against guns. Guns ignore *natural* Toughness (Force +3), not armor.

**Toughness section.** Add this table after the Toughness paragraph, with the sentence under it:

| Where your Toughness comes from | Blunt | Blades | Guns | Electricity, gas, fire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Force +3 or higher | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Kevlar vest | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Tactical armor, Armored power | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Toughness (street power) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Take the single highest row that applies to the attack, never two added together. A hero with Force +4 and a Kevlar vest has Toughness 1 against a punch and Toughness 1 against a bullet, never 2.

**Weapons table, Guns row.**
- Was: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better (a graze is just a graze) and ignores Toughness. Firing one is loud: +1 Heat"
- Now: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better (a graze is just a graze) and ignores natural Toughness, though armor still works. Firing one is loud: +1 Heat"

**Gear, "Gun rules (all guns)".**
- Was: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better, ignores Toughness, Disadvantage when you're engaged in melee, and +1 Heat for each fight where you fire one."
- Now: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better, ignores natural Toughness (Force +3) but not armor, Disadvantage when you're engaged in melee, and +1 Heat for each fight where you fire one."

**Fifth Street Saints stat block, pistol line.**
- Was: "One in each group carries a pistol (+1 Harm on Impact 2+, ignores Toughness, +1 Heat)."
- Now: "One in each group carries a pistol (+1 Harm on Impact 2+, ignores natural Toughness but not armor, +1 Heat)."

## 2. Enemy actions: lieutenants and several named enemies (Nov 11)

**Villains chapter, "Scaling to the party".** Add this bullet directly after "Villain turns":

- **Lieutenants and several named enemies:** a lieutenant gets one turn per round, however many heroes there are, even fighting alone. When a fight has more than one named enemy, each villain takes its own turns by the rule above and each lieutenant takes one; no villain counts as alone while another named enemy is in the fight. Spread all their turns through the round.

Unchanged and still in force (for reference): a named villain gets one turn per round for every two heroes, rounded up (one against 1 or 2 heroes, two against 3 or 4), and a villain fighting alone, with no mooks or lieutenants, gets one turn per hero.

**Design notes, "Open-questions" list.** Add this entry after the Nov 10 "Open-questions pass":

- **Several named enemies (Nov 11, Stephen):** a fight can have more than one villain or lieutenant. Villains keep the usual turns; a lieutenant always gets one turn per round. The simulator and the Encounter lab model this; a single villain's fight is unchanged.

## 3. Heroes with no civilian life (Nov 11)

**The clocks section.** Add this paragraph directly after the clocks table (after the Home row):

**Heroes with no civilian life.** A pure vigilante has no day job or alter ego. Their civilian life, and so Show Up and the Home clock, just stands for whatever anchors them to their humanity, even if it's only feeding a goldfish. Name it when you build the hero. When Home ticks, that anchor is what's slipping, and a Home crisis is that anchor being lost, found or threatened.

## 5. Powered armor (Nov 11)

A new piece of gear for heroes in advanced armor, such as a power suit. Add this row to the Toughness table in the Stats and defenses chapter, after "Tactical armor, Armored power":

| Where your Toughness comes from | Blunt | Blades | Guns | Electricity, gas, fire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Powered armor | 2 | 2 | 2 | 0 |

And add this row to the Gear table, after "Tactical armor":

- **Powered armor (takes 2 slots).** Toughness 2 against blunt, blades and guns. Unreliable: when you roll a Miss while wearing it, the GM can cut it out (out of power, a jammed joint, a short circuit, whatever fits the scene). While it is offline it gives no Toughness. Spend a Quick Action to get it running again, or it is repaired at Dawn. Doesn't stack with other Toughness.

Toughness still never goes above 2. The Armored power (strange) is unchanged.

## 7. Connections (Nov 11)

A new part of character creation. Add this as its own section in the Archetypes chapter, between "Flairs" and "Advancement":

**Connections**

Every hero is tied to the world by four connections, written at character creation, one line each. They are what the GM can lean on and what the player has to lose.

- **Someone you protect.** This is your stake. A person you would take a hit for, or can't afford to lose.
- **Someone with leverage over you.** A debt, a secret, a boss, a cop, a promise. This is the GM's lever.
- **A place you're tied to.** A block, a bar, a rooftop, a grave.
- **A mistake that could come back.** An old case gone wrong, someone you failed.

At least one connection should link you to another hero. At the table, ask: how do we know each other?

The GM uses connections for Impact 1 costs, Miss moves, Home crises (a loved one becomes a target, someone learns who you are) and personal clues, which already count double and tick your Home clock. When a connection is lost, resolved or betrayed, write a new one at the next Dawn.

A hero with no civilian life still has connections. Their "someone you protect" can be anything that anchors them to their humanity, even a goldfish.

## 8. Free weapons, the knife and Martial Artist (Nov 11)

Weapons no longer cost slots. Everyone carries a knife, plus one free weapon.

**Gear and Powers chapter, intro.** In "Spend them however fits the hero", the Daredevil line now reads "Two street powers and a free weapon is a Daredevil." Add this sentence before it: "Every hero also carries a free weapon and a knife (see below), which cost no slots."

**New section "Weapons you carry"**, between the slots text and "Gear". Add:

Everyone carries a **knife**, and every hero also gets **one free weapon** of their choice. Neither costs a slot. Each extra weapon costs 1 slot. A weapon can still be lost: disarming you is a fair GM move.

| Weapon | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Knife (everyone has one) | +1 Harm against anyone without Toughness. Can be thrown at Near range. It is a backup: each attack uses one weapon, never two weapon bonuses. |
| Sidearm (free pick) | A gun (Flow to shoot). Far range is at Disadvantage. |
| Long gun (free pick) | A gun with no Far penalty. Being seen carrying one is +1 Heat. |
| Light melee (free pick: baton, fighting sticks, weighted gloves) | +1 Harm on Impact 2 or better. Works with Strikes and Jabs. |
| Heavy melee (free pick: bat, pipe, crowbar) | +1 Harm on any hit, but only as a Full Action. |

A stranger weapon, like a mono-edge blade, shock gauntlets or a rail rifle, is the **Special Weapon** street power. Fighting with bare hands is the **Martial Artist** street power.

**Gear table.** Cross out the Sidearm, Long gun, Light melee, Heavy melee and Knife rows: they moved to the new section.

**Combat chapter, Weapons.** Add this line under the heading: "Everyone carries a knife and one free weapon (see Weapons you carry)."

## 9. Kit is now the utility belt; gear is armor and tools (Nov 11)

Kit no longer exists as a separate list. A Specialist's or Gadgeteer's utility belt holds ordinary gear, so there is one list of items instead of two. Kit items map to gear: Smoke is smoke grenades, Grapple is the grapple line, Flashbang is flashbangs, Taser is the taser, Tracker is the tracker (new gear), Armor is a Kevlar vest. "Long gun or rig" is gone: a long gun is a weapon now.

**Specialist section.** Replace the Drive trigger and the whole Kit entry (the Kit line and its seven sub-bullets) with:

- **Drive trigger:** you use a tool to change the fight (a new zone tag, a moved enemy, a blocked exit).
- **Utility belt:** 3 belt slots, on top of your 3 slots. At the start of each Night, fill them with tools or light armor (any one-slot gear that isn't a weapon). Belt gear works exactly like gear. Expecting guns? Take smoke grenades or a Kevlar vest: without them, a Specialist is usually the first one down.

**Gadgeteer, Start.**
- Was: "Utility belt. You get Kit: pick 2 Kit items per Night (using one is a Quick Action, once per item per fight). A Specialist, who already has Kit, picks 5 instead of 3."
- Now: "Utility belt. You get 2 belt slots: each Night, fill them with tools or light armor (any one-slot gear that isn't a weapon). A Specialist, who already has a belt, gets 5 belt slots instead of 3."

**Gear and Powers, intro.** "Specialists get their Kit on top of their slots." now reads: "Specialists and Gadgeteers also get a utility belt, which sits on top of their slots (see Specialist and Gadgeteer)."

**Gear section.** Replace the sentence under the Gear heading with: "Gear is armor and tools. Gear can be lost: smashing your gear, or catching you without it, is a fair GM move. A utility belt (Specialists and Gadgeteers) holds one-slot armor and tools, but never weapons or powered armor." Split the Gear table in two: put Kevlar vest, Tactical armor and Powered armor under a bold **Armor** heading (header row "Armor | Effect"), and the rest under a bold **Tools** heading (header row "Tool | Effect").

**More gear.** Rename "More gear" to **More tools** (header row "Tool | Effect"). Reword three rows so they no longer point to Kit:
- Taser: "A Jab. On a hit, the target loses their next Quick Action. Electricity, so it ignores Toughness. Two uses per fight."
- Smoke grenades: "Quick Action: a zone becomes Dark until the end of next round. Two per fight."
- Flashbangs: "Quick Action: roll Flow against the Guard of everyone in a zone; everyone you beat has Disadvantage on their next roll. One per fight."

Add one new row to that table, before Pepper spray:

| Tool | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Tracker | Tag someone. If they get away, take 1 clue about where they went. |

**Sample loadouts, Patchwork.** "(plus Kit)" is now "(plus a utility belt)".

One effect to know: Kit was once per item per fight. Belt gear uses its own limits, so a taser or smoke grenades on the belt gets two uses per fight.
