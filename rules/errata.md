# Errata: changes since the printed rules

Changes made to the rules since the printed copy, each with where to find it and the exact wording. The printed copy is the version headed "Nov 9, 2026" (first published Nov 10, 2026). If yours is newer, skip any entry it already has. The current rules are always the Rules link in the footer.

## 1. Guns and armor (Nov 10)

Armor now works against guns. Guns ignore *natural* Toughness (Force +3), not armor.

**Toughness section.** Add this table after the Toughness paragraph, with the sentence under it:

| Where your Toughness comes from | Blunt | Blades | Guns | Electricity, gas, fire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Force +3 or higher | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Kevlar vest | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Tactical armor, Armored power | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Toughness (street power) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Take the single highest row that applies to the attack, never two added together. A hero with Force +4 and a Kevlar vest has Toughness 1 against a punch and Toughness 1 against a bullet, never 2.

**Weapons table, Guns row.**
- Was: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better (a graze is just a graze) and ignores Toughness. Firing one is loud: +1 Heat"
- Now: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better (a graze is just a graze) and ignores natural Toughness, though armor still works. Firing one is loud: +1 Heat"

**Gear, "Gun rules (all guns)".**
- Was: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better, ignores Toughness, Disadvantage when you're engaged in melee, and +1 Heat for each fight where you fire one."
- Now: "+1 Harm on Impact 2 or better, ignores natural Toughness (Force +3) but not armor, Disadvantage when you're engaged in melee, and +1 Heat for each fight where you fire one."

**Fifth Street Saints stat block, pistol line.**
- Was: "One in each group carries a pistol (+1 Harm on Impact 2+, ignores Toughness, +1 Heat)."
- Now: "One in each group carries a pistol (+1 Harm on Impact 2+, ignores natural Toughness but not armor, +1 Heat)."

## 2. Enemy actions: lieutenants and several named enemies (Nov 11)

**Villains chapter, "Scaling to the party".** Add this bullet directly after "Villain turns":

- **Lieutenants and several named enemies:** a lieutenant gets one turn per round, however many heroes there are, even fighting alone. When a fight has more than one named enemy, each villain takes its own turns by the rule above and each lieutenant takes one; no villain counts as alone while another named enemy is in the fight. Spread all their turns through the round.

Unchanged and still in force (for reference): a named villain gets one turn per round for every two heroes, rounded up (one against 1 or 2 heroes, two against 3 or 4), and a villain fighting alone, with no mooks or lieutenants, gets one turn per hero.

**Design notes, "Open-questions" list.** Add this entry after the Nov 10 "Open-questions pass":

- **Several named enemies (Nov 11, Stephen):** a fight can have more than one villain or lieutenant. Villains keep the usual turns; a lieutenant always gets one turn per round. The simulator and the Encounter lab model this; a single villain's fight is unchanged.

## 3. Heroes with no civilian life (Nov 11)

**The clocks section.** Add this paragraph directly after the clocks table (after the Home row):

**Heroes with no civilian life.** A pure vigilante has no day job or alter ego. Their civilian life, and so Show Up and the Home clock, just stands for whatever anchors them to their humanity, even if it's only feeding a goldfish. Name it when you build the hero. When Home ticks, that anchor is what's slipping, and a Home crisis is that anchor being lost, found or threatened.

## 4. The Toughness power is now Resilience (Nov 11)

Renamed so it can't be confused with the Toughness you get from Force +3. Nothing about how it works has changed. Change every mention of the street power on your print:

- **Powers chapter heading:** "Toughness (street power; hard to hurt)" is now **Resilience (street power; hard to hurt)**.
- **Toughness table (Stats and defenses):** the row "Toughness (street power)" is now **Resilience (street power)**.
- **Sample heroes table, Bulwark:** "Toughness (source: years of hauling hoses), med kit, riot shield" is now "Resilience (source: years of hauling hoses), med kit, riot shield".
- **Design notes, list of powers that have seen play:** "Shadow Manipulation, Toughness, Invulnerability..." is now "Shadow Manipulation, Resilience, Invulnerability...".
- **Resilience, "Tough" ability.**
  - Was: "...you have Toughness 1 against fists and blunt weapons. This doesn't stack with Toughness from Force +3."
  - Now: "...you have Toughness 1 against fists and blunt weapons. This doesn't stack with Toughness from Force +3 or from any other source: use your single highest."
