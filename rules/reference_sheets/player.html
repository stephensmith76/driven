The core roll

2d10 + stat vs a defense (12 + a stat)

Margin = your total minus the defense.

MarginResultIn a fightOutside a fight
Below 0MissGM makes a moveFails or goes wrong; GM makes a move
0 to 3Impact 11 Harm or the effect. No costYou get it, with a cost: GM offers two, you pick one
4 to 7Impact 22 HarmClean success
8+Impact 33 HarmSuccess plus a bonus (a clue, a position, an opening)
  • Attacks never carry a cost. Every other Impact 1 does, in or out of a fight.
  • Impact tops out at 3. Weapon, power and Signature bonuses add Harm, not Impact. Anything that triggers on Impact 3 needs a real Impact 3 or a Drive Upgrade.
  • Edge: +2 to the roll. One Edge per roll, whatever the source.
  • Advantage / Disadvantage: roll 3d10, keep the best (worst) two. Any of each cancels; they never stack. Edge and Advantage can both apply.
  • No real opposition and failure isn't interesting? Don't roll.

Harm: 4 boxes

BoxWhat it means
BruisedNo effect. Clears at the end of every scene.
HurtNo effect yet. One box from real trouble.
WoundedDisadvantage on Force and Flow rolls.
DownOut of the fight. Talk, crawl, or one desperate Quick Action a round at Disadvantage. Or spend the whole turn on Second Wind.
  • Dying: hit again while Down (one hit always stops at Down). An ally's Full Action in your zone stabilizes you.
  • Death is the table's call. Unstabilized at the scene's end, the player decides: the story ends, or you're left for dead (Down until Dawn, tick Home twice).
  • Grit your teeth: at the start of your turn, a Wounded hero takes 1 Stress to ignore the Wounded Disadvantage until their next turn. Not at 8 Stress.
  • Enemies who drop are out of the fight, not dead, unless the table agrees otherwise.

Recovery

  • Breather: Bruised clears at the end of every scene.
  • Down after a fight: back up at Wounded when it ends. A stabilized Dying hero stays Down until Dawn (needs Patch Up and Sleep It Off).
  • Dying leaves a mark: start the next Night with a Harm box Patch Up can't clear (a med kit, healing power, Lair or clinic can).
  • Second Wind or a med kit gets a Downed hero up at Wounded mid-fight.

Connections

Four, one line each: someone you protect, someone with leverage over you, a place you're tied to, a mistake that could come back. Link at least one to another hero. The first time each scene you take Harm or Stress, or real risk, to protect one: +1 Drive. Lost one? Write a new one at the next Dawn.

Building a hero

  1. Stats: Specialist (+4, +2, +1, -1) or Balanced (+3, +2, +1, 0).
  2. Trait (one of twelve, or your own with the GM).
  3. Archetype.
  4. One Flair.
  5. Four connections.
  6. Free weapon, plus a knife.
  7. Three slots: gear, a street power or a strange power.
  8. Anchor for Home: your civilian life.
  9. Neighborhood: where, who runs it, what's wrong there.
  10. Team pick (optional): a Lair or one Asset, chosen together.

You gain Stress when:

  • you Strain for an extra Quick Action (1)
  • a Presence attack hits you (1 per Impact)
  • someone you're protecting is hurt or dies on your watch (1 to 2)
  • the GM makes a hard move against you (1, on top of the move)
  • you act against your Trait (1)
  • the Scheme clock ticks: on your roll, you take 1; at Dawn or a missed Crack, everyone does
  • you go Down (2); you see an ally go Down (1, first time each fight)
  • an Impact 1 cost, when it fits (never at 8 Stress)

While Down, you take no Stress from outside sources.

The hook: while you're Fraying, the GM can push a complication through your hook. Go along with it: +1 Drive. Refuse: 1 Stress.

After Breaking: at the scene's end, drop to 4 Stress, gain 2 Drive, tick Home once.

Strain: once per turn, 1 Stress for an extra Quick Action.

Fights: your turn

Turn order: sides alternate, one hero then one enemy (villain or mook group). Players pick which hero goes next. Heroes go first each round, unless ambushed (then the enemy takes round 1's first turn). The ambusher's first roll has Edge.

One free move + one Full Action or two Quick Actions

Free move: to an adjacent zone (you can engage one enemy there), or into or out of engagement in your zone.

Full Actions

  • Strike: attack with Force, Flow or Presence. Normal Impact. Heavy melee is a Strike with Force.
  • Signature Move: 3 Drive.
  • Stabilize: save a Dying ally in your zone.
  • Objective step: a roll against a rating, where the step is.

Quick Actions

  • Jab: attack at -2 (none with light melee). A hit is always Impact 1, so Impact 2+ bonuses never apply. Once per turn.
  • Move: go one more zone.
  • Help: an ally in your zone gets Edge on their next roll.
  • Take Cover: in a Cover zone, +2 Guard until your next turn.
  • Interact: pick up, open, throw a switch, kick a gun away.
  • Vanish (Stalker): in Dark, Cover or Crowd, Flow vs the best watching Wits to go Hidden.
  • Archetype Quick Actions (on your sheet).

Strain: 1 Stress, one extra Quick Action, once per turn.

Breaking away: leave engagement and that enemy gets a free Jab at you.

Leaving a fight: from a zone with an exit, not engaged, spend your turn.

Maneuvers

Grab, disarm, shove or trip someone you're engaged with. Full Action, or a Quick Action at -2 like a Jab (a hit counts as Impact 1). Force to grab or shove, Flow to trip or disarm, vs their Guard. No Harm. A hit takes a mook out.

Objectives: any hero can take any step (Full Action, a roll vs -1, +1 or +3). Impact 2+ finishes it (Impact 3: Edge on the next step); Impact 1 finishes it with a cost. Not while engaged, unless the step is about that enemy. Finishing the Objective wins the fight.

Defeat: all heroes Down or fled. Scheme ticks twice, each hero ticks Home or Heat.

Chases and vehicles

Chase: each round the chasers pick one hero to roll Flow vs the quarry's Guard (or a rating). Three hits catch them (Impact 3 counts as two); three Misses and they're gone. Running away works the same, vs the pursuers' Wits. Tailing someone is a chase.

Vehicles: a moving vehicle is a zone, the road a traffic Hazard. The driver spends a Quick Action each turn. Jumping between vehicles: Flow vs -1, +1 or +3. Ramming: Full Action, vehicle rating (+1 bike, +3 car) vs Guard, counts as heavy melee, Impact 2+ shoves them a zone.

Between cases

Harm clears. Stress above 3 drops to 3. Drive resets to 2. Heat drops by 2. Home stays. Each hero takes one Advancement: +1 to a stat, a Flair, the next power ability, a new slot, or a Lair room or Asset.

The Night

Patrol → Confrontation → Dawn

Following a lead

Describe your approach, roll the stat that fits vs the lead's rating. You get the clue no matter what.

ResultYou get
MissThe clue; GM picks a cost (Scheme tick, +1 Heat, 1 Stress, or an ambush)
Impact 1The clue; GM offers two costs, you pick one
Impact 2The clue, clean
Impact 3The clue plus one: a second clue, Edge on one roll against the villain (any roll that fairly counts), or a hint at their Weakness

The team gets one no-roll clue per Night from all sources (Work the Case, Plugs, a hacking rig...). Extra ones give Edge on a lead instead.

Cracking the case

With clues at least half the Complexity and a theory answering who, what, where: roll 2d10 + clues used vs 10 + Complexity. Personal clues count as 2 (and tick your Home).

MissWrong. Regroup (Scheme ticks twice) or Go in anyway (ticks once, into a trap)
Impact 1Right, with a snag
Impact 2Right
Impact 3Right, plus the villain's Weakness or an arc clue

Whose ground? Crack at Impact 2+: you pick (ambush, or add a zone tag). Impact 1: no ambush, villain +1 Drive. A trap or full Scheme: the villain picks and ambushes.

Dawn: pick two

First the Scheme ticks, and every hero not Down takes 1 Stress.

  • Sleep It Off: clear 3 Stress.
  • Patch Up: clear Hurt and Wounded. With Sleep It Off, clears Down.
  • Show Up: a scene with a connection, clear 1 Stress. Skip it and tick Home.
  • Lay Low: clear 1 Heat.
  • Work the Case: 1 clue, no roll (the team's no-roll clue; if used, Edge on a lead next Night).
  • Train (Gym Lair only): start the next Night with 1 more Drive.
